Bubbles

A Gumball for Your Thoughts

Bubble Gum robot, or “Bubbles” for short, is from a family of track-footed robots that originated from an experiment melding candy vending machines with robotics in the early 1980s. She's a favorite of Binaryville.

Fred

It’s All 0’s and 1’s to Me!

Fred is inquisitive and creative, and always conjuring up ways to improve Binaryville. He has a special affinity for improving the world through alternative energy and green-living.

Rivet

All-Weather is My Jam

Rivet descended from an assembly line of amphibious robots, but never quite took to deep water - though he does love rolling around in mucky marshes and soaking up the mud on vacation.

Dolly

You've Got This!

Dolly is Binaryville's local fitness guru with the huge "can-do" attitude. She's a popular personal trainer who's inspired many folks to improve their lives through exercise and healthful eating.

Cosmo

Find Me at Binaryville Beach

For an esteemed aeronautical engineer who manned the first ever flight to Mars from Binaryville, Cosmo is one of the most down-to-earth folks you'll ever meet. He loves playing bingo and reading spy novels.

Elton

Lights, Camera, Action!

Elton’s a key grip in the Binaryville movie industry, where he’s known for his expert lighting and camera operation. He’s also experienced in front of the camera and enjoys taking acting lessons.
